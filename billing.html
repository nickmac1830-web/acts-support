Billing Terms

ACTS Pro subscription and purchase information.

ACTS Pro Subscriptions

ACTS may offer Weekly, Monthly and Annual auto-renewable ACTS Pro subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Payment

Payment is charged to the user's Apple account at confirmation of purchase. ACTS does not directly process or store payment card information.

Automatic Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically for the selected billing period unless cancelled through the user's Apple account settings in accordance with Apple's applicable subscription rules.

Cancellation

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; access generally continues until the end of the current paid subscription period, subject to Apple's policies.

Restore Purchases

Users with an existing ACTS Pro entitlement can use the Restore Purchases option inside ACTS to ask Apple's purchasing system to restore eligible purchases.

Pricing and Availability

Prices and availability may vary by storefront and will be shown by Apple before purchase.
