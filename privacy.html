Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

ACTS: Auctioneer Training (“ACTS”) is an auctioneer training and simulation application operated by Nicholas McIntyre. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ACTS.

Information ACTS Processes

ACTS does not require users to create an ACTS account. ACTS does not ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, camera access or advertising identifier as part of normal app use.

If you contact ACTS for support, ACTS will receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your support request.

Training Information

Training information generated through use of ACTS may include auction scripts, practice results, scores, bid history, timing information, streaks, settings and preferences.

This training information is stored locally on the user's device. ACTS does not transmit this information to an ACTS-operated server or provide it to RevenueCat, advertisers or data brokers.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

ACTS includes an optional voice-control feature that can recognise spoken bids and auction commands. If the user chooses to enable this feature, ACTS requests permission to access the device's microphone and speech-recognition capability.

Voice recognition is configured to use Apple's on-device speech-recognition technology. Raw microphone recordings are not retained by ACTS and are not uploaded to an ACTS server, RevenueCat or a third-party advertising service. Recognised commands are used on the device to operate the auction session. The resulting bid or auction action may appear in the locally stored session history.

Microphone access is optional. Users may decline or revoke microphone and speech-recognition permission through their device settings. ACTS can still be used with its standard manual controls when voice control is unavailable or disabled.

Text-to-Speech

ACTS may generate spoken auction-training responses using locally bundled text-to-speech technology. Text-to-speech processing is performed on the user's device and does not require ACTS to upload the user's training history or auction scripts to an external speech provider.

Subscriptions and Purchases

ACTS Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. ACTS does not receive or store users' full payment-card or bank-account details. Purchases are subject to Apple's applicable payment, security and privacy practices.

ACTS uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription-management provider, to validate purchases and manage ACTS Pro access. Apple and RevenueCat may process an anonymous or pseudonymous app-user identifier, product identifiers, purchase history, transaction and subscription status, transaction dates, receipt or signed verification information, and entitlement status.

This limited subscription information is used to validate purchases, prevent fraud, provide and restore ACTS Pro access, and understand subscription performance. ACTS does not send locally stored auction or training history to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Analytics and Advertising

ACTS does not use third-party advertising networks or behavioural-tracking services. ACTS does not sell personal information and does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

RevenueCat provides limited subscription analytics relating to purchases, subscription status and ACTS Pro entitlements. This information is used for app functionality and subscription analysis, not for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Data Sharing

ACTS does not sell or rent personal information and does not share locally stored auction or training information with advertisers or data brokers. Apple processes information required to provide App Store and payment services. RevenueCat processes the limited subscription information described above as a service provider to ACTS.

Apple and RevenueCat may process information in countries outside Australia in accordance with their respective privacy and security practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Training information remains locally on the user's device until it is removed through controls provided within ACTS, the app's local data is cleared, or ACTS is removed from the device.

Because ACTS does not maintain a remote account database containing users' training information, ACTS cannot remotely access or delete that locally stored information.

Deleting ACTS does not automatically cancel an active subscription or remove purchase records maintained by Apple or RevenueCat. Subscriptions can be managed through the user's Apple account at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions .

Security

ACTS uses the security and application-sandboxing protections provided by iOS to protect information stored locally on the device. No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

ACTS is an auctioneer-training tool and is not directed to children under 13. ACTS does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

Users may control microphone and speech-recognition access through their device settings, remove locally stored training information through ACTS, uninstall the app, and manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple account.

Privacy or subscription-information enquiries may be submitted using the contact details below.
