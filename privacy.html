Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026

ACTS: Auctioneer Training (“ACTS”) is designed to provide auctioneer training and simulation tools while respecting user privacy.

Information We Collect

ACTS does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone access, or an advertising identifier.

Training Data

Training information generated through use of the app, including training results, scores, history and streak information, is stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to ACTS or stored on external servers.

Analytics and Advertising

ACTS does not use third-party advertising services or behavioural-tracking services. RevenueCat provides subscription analytics relating to purchases and ACTS Pro entitlements, as described below. This information is not used by ACTS for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Subscriptions and Purchases

ACTS Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. ACTS does not receive or store users' full payment-card or bank-account details. Purchases are subject to Apple's applicable payment, security and privacy practices.

ACTS uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription-management provider, to validate purchases and manage ACTS Pro access. Apple and RevenueCat may process an anonymous app-user identifier, product identifiers, purchase history, transaction and subscription status, relevant dates, receipt or signed verification data, and entitlement status.

This limited subscription information is used to validate purchases, prevent fraud, provide and restore ACTS Pro access, and provide subscription analytics. ACTS does not send locally stored auction or training history to RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Third-Party Data Sharing

ACTS does not sell or rent personal information and does not share locally stored auction or training information with advertisers or other third parties. Apple processes information required to provide App Store services, and RevenueCat processes the limited subscription information described above as a service provider to ACTS.

Children's Privacy

ACTS does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because ACTS training information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to ACTS servers, ACTS does not maintain a remote database of user training information. Locally stored information can be removed through controls provided within the app or by removing the app from the device, as applicable.

Deleting ACTS does not cancel an active subscription or remove purchase records maintained by Apple or RevenueCat. Subscriptions can be managed through the user's Apple account. Users may contact ACTS using the details below with questions about subscription information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ACTS's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available through this page.
