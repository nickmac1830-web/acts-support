Subscriptions

ACTS Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

  • Weekly, Monthly and Annual ACTS Pro plans may be available depending on your App Store region.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your Apple account settings.
  • Use Restore Purchases inside ACTS if you have an active subscription that is not being recognised.

Training Data

Training scores, history and streak information are stored locally on the user's device. ACTS does not operate a remote account system for this information.
